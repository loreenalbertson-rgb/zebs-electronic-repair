Workshop policies

Clear expectations protect everyone.

These policies are written as a practical customer framework for an independent electronics repair workshop. A specific estimate, receipt, or written agreement can add project specific terms.

01

Repair service & authorization

Submitting a website request does not mean a device has been accepted. Zeb reviews the device type, reported problem, safety concerns, tools required, parts availability, and current technical scope before arranging drop off.

Once a device is accepted for diagnosis, the customer authorizes reasonable non destructive inspection and testing needed to evaluate the reported problem. Any repair, parts order, upgrade, or additional labor beyond the agreed diagnostic scope requires customer approval first.

Training disclosure

Zeb is currently completing electronics focused training through Penn Foster. Service offerings are intentionally limited to work that fits current skill, tools, and safety requirements. Completion or certification is not claimed before it is officially earned.

02

Diagnostics, estimates & parts

The website displays introductory starting labor prices. These are not final quotes because the same symptom can have several different causes.

  • The standard diagnostic fee covers an accepted initial bench assessment.
  • Under the current introductory pricing model, that fee is credited toward repair labor when the recommended paid repair is approved.
  • Parts, shipping, specialty materials, severe damage, and advanced labor are additional unless included in the written quote.
  • No parts should be ordered for a customer job until the customer approves the part and expected cost.
  • If the device condition changes the scope of work, Zeb should stop and request new approval before continuing.
03

Data, passwords & device access

Customers are responsible for backing up important data before service whenever possible. Electronics repair can involve resets, drive removal, replacement hardware, operating system access, or unexpected component failure. No repair service should be treated as a guaranteed data preservation service.

If a password, PIN, or account access is needed for testing, the customer should provide only what is necessary for the repair and change it after service. Zeb should not browse personal files except when directly necessary to verify the requested repair or when specifically authorized by the customer.

Devices containing highly sensitive personal, business, financial, medical, or legal information should be backed up and appropriately secured before handoff. Data recovery is outside the standard service scope unless specifically agreed in writing.

04

Workmanship & warranty framework

Any warranty that applies to a specific repair should be stated on the final service record. If no warranty is written for a project, the customer should not assume a particular coverage period.

A workmanship warranty, when offered, should apply only to the exact repair performed. It should not cover unrelated failures, new damage, impact, liquid exposure, power surge, misuse, tampering after service, normal wear, software problems unrelated to the repair, or a different component failure.

Manufacturer warranties on replacement parts, when available, are separate from workshop labor and depend on the supplier's terms.

05

Customer supplied parts

Zeb may decline customer supplied parts that appear incompatible, unsafe, counterfeit, damaged, or unsuitable for the repair. When a customer provides a part, the workshop cannot guarantee the quality, authenticity, lifespan, or manufacturer warranty of that part.

Labor charges may still apply when a customer supplied part fails to solve the reported problem, because installation and diagnostic time were still performed.

06

Electronics donation terms

Accepted electronics donations are different from customer repair devices. Unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, an accepted donation transfers ownership of the device and included parts to Zeb's Electronics Repair.

Donated electronics may be opened, tested, erased, disassembled, repaired, modified, used for parts, used for soldering or troubleshooting practice, refurbished, resold, given away, or recycled depending on condition and usefulness.

  • Donations are not guaranteed to be repaired.
  • Donations are not normally returned.
  • Donors should remove accounts, activation locks, memory cards, SIM cards, and personal data before handoff whenever possible.
  • A donor may state a preference against future resale. Zeb can then decide whether the donation still fits the workshop's needs.
  • This is an independent for profit workshop, not a charitable organization. The website does not represent electronics donations as charitable tax deductible gifts.
07

Safety limits & excluded work

The workshop may refuse any device that creates unreasonable safety, legal, contamination, fire, electrical, or handling risk. Current exclusions include:

  • Swollen, punctured, leaking, smoking, unusually hot, or otherwise unstable batteries.
  • CRT televisions and CRT monitors.
  • Medical, life supporting, emergency, alarm, or life safety equipment.
  • High voltage equipment outside current training and safe bench capability.
  • Devices contaminated with unknown hazardous materials, pests, biological material, or severe liquid contamination.
  • Projects requiring manufacturer authorization, specialized calibration, regulated credentials, or tools not currently available.

Even if a device type is normally accepted, Zeb may decline a specific project when the fault is beyond current scope.

08

Website privacy

This GitHub Pages site is designed as a static website. The intake form prepares a message in the visitor's browser and does not store form submissions in a custom website database.

If a contact email is configured, submitting the form opens the visitor's email application with the prepared request. The email provider used by the customer and the email provider used by Zeb may process that message under their own privacy practices.

Before accepting paid work

Business registration, taxes, insurance, local operating rules, customer receipts, payment processing, and any legally required disclosures should be verified for the final business setup. This website copy is an operational starting point and is not a substitute for legal or tax advice.
